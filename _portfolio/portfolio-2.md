A short paper investigating the convergence and surjectivity of the matrix exponential and matrix logarithm using complex analysis and linear algebra. In particular, we show that the matrix exponential maps surjectively from the Lie Algebra of //(SO(3)//) into SO(3) and surjectively from the set of n x n Complex Matrices into GL(n). This paper is meant to be an analysis-motivated introduction to Lie Groups through the exponential map.
